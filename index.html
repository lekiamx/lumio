✅ No ads · No tracking · No subscriptions

BrightSpark ("the App") is a children's educational app developed by BrightSpark Kids ("we", "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially minors.

1. Data we do NOT collect

BrightSpark does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, including:

  • Child's name, age, or identifying information
  • Geographic location
  • Contacts, photos, or device files
  • Email addresses
  • Behavioral data or analytics
  • Personalized advertising or cross-app tracking

2. Data stored locally on device

The App stores the following game data locally on the user's device (using Hive, a local database):

  • Player profiles (name and avatar chosen by the user)
  • Game progress, scores, and earned stars
  • Audio and language preferences
  • Parental control state (PIN stored locally, encrypted)

This data never leaves the device. It is not synced to any external server and is not shared with third parties.

3. In-app purchases

BrightSpark offers an optional one-time purchase of USD $2.99 to unlock all premium content. This transaction is processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not receive or store credit card or payment information.

RevenueCat may collect an anonymous device identifier to validate purchase status. See their policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. COPPA and children's privacy compliance

This App is designed for children. We comply with COPPA and equivalent international regulations:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13
  • We do not display any advertising
  • We do not allow external communication or social networks within the App
  • Parental controls (PIN) are managed exclusively by adults

5. Device permissions

BrightSpark requests only strictly necessary permissions:

  • Audio — to play sound effects and in-game music
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback in games (optional)

We do not request access to camera, microphone, contacts, location, or external storage.

6. Third-party services

  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management (no children's personal data)
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing (managed by Google)

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will post the updated version at this same URL and update the "last updated" date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact us
